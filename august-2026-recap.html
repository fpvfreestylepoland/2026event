FPV Silesia Poland ’26 Summer Edition

A casual FPV freestyle meetup for pilots from across the Silesia region — one full day of flying, gear talk and good company at the LKS Tempo Stolarzowice sports complex in Bytom.

This page is a recap for our partners and sponsors: who showed up, the recap video, and every photo from the day. The next edition — Autumn Edition — takes place in October 2026 at the same venue.
