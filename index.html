About the event

FPV Freestyle is the art of flying drones from a first-person perspective, blending style, flow, and creativity. This event is for FPV enthusiasts who want to meet up in Silesia, share experiences, talk about gear, and fly together.

Expect adrenaline, creativity, and a great atmosphere — plus exciting contests with prizes from our sponsors.

Contest Rules

Show off your style and win prizes from top FPV brands. We've prepared two independent contests for you: a video contest (before the event) and a surprise contest on site.

Video Contest (Offline)

Submit your video before May 20, 2026. The jury will pick winners in the Freestyle and Cinematic categories, and prizes will be awarded during the event.

On-site Contest (Live)

During the event we'll announce an extra challenge! You — the pilots and attendees — will vote for the person whose on-site skills impress you the most.

How to enter the video contest

1

Record and Choose

Prepare footage in the Freestyle or Cinematic category. You can submit multiple videos (no limit) — pick your best flights and shots.

2

Submit Your Entry

Upload your video to Drive/OneDrive and send the link to: fpvfreestylepoland@gmail.com. Let us know whether it's a Freestyle or Cinematic submission. Deadline: May 20, 2026.

3

Prizes

During the event the jury will announce the video contest results. Winners will receive prizes provided by our sponsors.

Technical Tips

  • Quality: Send files in the best possible quality (4K preferred). Avoid social-media compression.
  • Music (if any): Make sure you have the rights to use the soundtrack (or use Royalty Free tracks).
  • Length: We recommend videos between 30 seconds and 3:00 minutes long.
  • Editing: For Cinematic entries, raw skill isn't the only thing that counts — editing concept and audio sync matter too.
