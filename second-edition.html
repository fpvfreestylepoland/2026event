02 · Event Format

Three competition categories

Unlike traditional drone racing, this event focuses on creativity, precision and freestyle flying.

Flagship

5" Freestyle

The flagship category. Pilots perform freestyle runs scored on five dimensions.

  • Creativity
  • Flow
  • Difficulty
  • Precision
  • Overall impression
Lightweight

3.5" Freestyle

Dedicated to lightweight freestyle drones. Identical criteria, with emphasis on technical flying in tighter spaces.

  • Creativity
  • Flow
  • Difficulty
  • Precision
  • Overall impression
Cinematic

Cinematic Flight

A separate category focused on smooth flying and visual storytelling.

  • Camera movement
  • Smoothness
  • Line selection
  • Composition
  • Overall cinematic quality
