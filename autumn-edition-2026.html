Who you are reaching

A room full of people who buy this gear

This is not a general public audience that happens to see a logo. Everyone on that field builds, flies, crashes and re-buys FPV hardware — frames, motors, flight controllers, VTXs, props, batteries, chargers, radios and goggles — several times a season.

🛠️

Active builders

Pilots who solder their own quads and pick components deliberately. They notice which brand is on the field and which one is not.

🗣️

Regional opinion leaders

Many of them run their own YouTube and Instagram channels and post their own footage from the day — extra reach we do not even count.

🌱

Newcomers

First-timers deciding what to buy next. Whatever they hold and fly at the meetup shapes that decision.

👨‍👩‍👧

Friends and family

Pilots bring people along, kids try the goggles, and the meetup pulls in curious visitors from around Bytom all day.

Geographically: pilots come from across Silesia — Poland’s most densely populated region — and from neighbouring voivodeships. Our channels are YouTube and Instagram; current channel figures are available on request.

About the event

It is not a competition — that is the point

FPV freestyle is the art of flying a drone from a first-person view, blending style, flow and creativity. FPV Silesia Poland is an informal gathering of pilots from across the region who want to fly together, swap experience and talk gear.

There is no scoreboard and no pressure, so nobody is rushing between heats. People arrive in the morning, set up their pits, fly, fix, eat and stay — which is exactly why a sponsor’s banner, product or giveaway gets seen properly instead of being glanced at between rounds.

Every pilot flies at their own responsibility and under Polish aviation regulations; the full event rules are published on the site and accepted at registration.
